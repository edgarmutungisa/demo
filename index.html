Try DST Uganda: it tells farmers what is likely next

The DST Uganda pilot serves Isingiro and Kasese districts. You are Sarah, a pastoralist with Ankole-cross cows for milk, who also grows maize and beans. DST Uganda only needs your name and your farm's coordinates (your phone's GPS, typed numbers or a Google Maps link); everything else is worked out from that point. You're chatting on WhatsApp. Your neighbour Okello has a basic phone and uses SMS. DST Uganda is a predictive tool: it forecasts the season, the start of the rains, grass and feed for the herd, disease risk and prices, with a probability and an action. Ask the way farmers really type, including short, half-finished questions.
